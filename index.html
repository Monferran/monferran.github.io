Research

One-loop spectra under T-duality

I’m looking at when T-duality preserves the one-loop energy of a folded string. I’m writing up the Klebanov–Witten case with Martin Schvellinger and David Jorrín.

AdS/CFT at zero radius

I’m exploring how free N = 4 super Yang–Mills theory is related to type IIB strings on AdS5 × S5 in the zero-radius limit.
