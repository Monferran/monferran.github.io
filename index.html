Research

One-loop spectra under T-duality

With Martin Schvellinger and David Jorrín · Manuscript in preparation

For folded strings in the Klebanov–Witten background, we compare the bosonic and fermionic fluctuation operators before and after Abelian and non-Abelian T-duality. We derive conditions for the periodic spectra and one-loop energies to agree, including compatibility of the boundary conditions and spin structures.

AdS/CFT at zero radius

I’m investigating a gauged linear sigma model for type IIB strings on AdS5 × S5 in the zero-radius limit. The question is how to recover the local operators and correlators of free N = 4 super Yang–Mills theory from the worldsheet.

Thesis

One-Loop Semiclassical Quantization in Holographic Backgrounds of Type IIB Superstring Theory

Licenciatura thesis, 2026 · Advisor: Martin Schvellinger · Revised November 2026 · In Spanish

In my thesis I computed the leading quantum correction to the energies of folded strings in AdS5 × S5, AdS5 × T1,1, and AdS5–Schwarzschild × S5. I expanded the Green–Schwarz action around the classical solutions and evaluated the fluctuation determinants from their monodromies using Forman’s method.
