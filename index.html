Research

Manuscript in preparation

One-loop spectra under T-duality

With Martin Schvellinger and David Jorrín, I study when Abelian and non-Abelian T-duality preserve periodic fluctuation spectra and one-loop string energies. We apply the result to folded strings in Klebanov–Witten holography.

Independent research

AdS/CFT at zero radius

I’m investigating a gauged linear sigma model for type IIB strings on AdS5 × S5 in the zero-radius limit and its relation to free 𝒩 = 4 super Yang–Mills theory.

Particle tracking in granular media

At the Faculty of Engineering at UBA, I developed a YOLO-based tracking pipeline to measure how grains mix in a two-dimensional periodic mixer.

About

I live in Buenos Aires. I completed my Licenciatura in Physics at the Universidad de Buenos Aires in 2026 and did my thesis research at the Instituto de Física La Plata (IFLP, CONICET–UNLP).

In July 2026, I presented a poster on the semiclassical quantization of rotating strings at Advances in Quantum Gravity & Strings in La Plata. I also attended the Perimeter–SAIFR–IFT Journeys into Theoretical Physics in São Paulo.

Outside physics, I play chess. I’ve been vegan for ten years.
