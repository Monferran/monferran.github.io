I’m interested in string theory, holography, and quantum field theory. I studied physics at the Universidad de Buenos Aires and did my thesis research at the Instituto de Física La Plata.

Research

One-loop spectra under T-duality

With Martin Schvellinger and David Jorrín, I study how T-duality acts on the fluctuation spectra of folded strings in the Klebanov–Witten background.

AdS/CFT at zero radius

I’m investigating a gauged linear sigma model for the zero-radius limit of type IIB strings on AdS5 × S5 and its relation to free N = 4 super Yang–Mills theory.
